M_02: bend-frame v0.2, the performance milestone (combined plan)
2026-10-11. Merged from plans/REVIEW_PERF_FABLE.txt and plans/REVIEW_PERF_ASTRA.txt
by Fable 5.1; revised by Astra after cross-reading both reviews.
Baseline: HEAD 3612482 (v0.1 release candidate; make ci green, 158 tests, 149 laws). The Bend author's charge ("linked lists for everything, needless
clones, parallelizing the wrong way") is the brief; both reviews found it true on
specific paths and false on others, and agree on where.

=====================================================================================
0. Objective and gates
=====================================================================================
Make bend-frame fast the Bend way, without giving up what v0.1 established: one
data semantics, the exact differential suite, the proof chain and --verdict.

Candidate release claim (only publish the parts demonstrated by the final gates):
"Owned chunks and fused kernels reduce copying and improve measured throughput;
parallel execution preserves the documented semantics on the supported paths."
Do not promise that every kernel forks, or that hashing/CSV is unconditionally
linear. Small inputs, skew, long records, hash collisions and F32 ordering matter.

Gates (W0 freezes the workloads, measurement procedure and thresholds):
  G1  Correctness: make ci green; old/new executor differential on Raw, Dictionary,
      deep, joinstress and CSV cases; at least 10,000 release cases across seeds,
      plus interpreter/native comparison. Compare typed values, validity, ordered
      names, height, row order and errors. F32 is bit-equal to v0.1 except the
      EXISTING documented NaN-payload relaxation; no new tolerance or exclusions.
  G2  Scaling: target >=3x compute speedup at 12 vs 1 thread on q1-q6, and >=4x
      parse speedup. W0 must establish per-query feasibility, including serial
      fractions. If a serial fraction exceeds 1/3, even infinite parallelism
      cannot provide 3x. Freeze justified per-workload release thresholds before
      implementation; do not silently relax them at W8. Report every query,
      including regressions; a geometric mean cannot hide a failing required cell.
      Balanced compute-bound chunk probes should demonstrate >=3x; serial F32,
      radix/scatter stages, bandwidth-bound and skewed work may scale less.
  G3  Aspirational 1-thread targets, confirmed or revised explicitly at W0:
      q1 <=3 ms, q2 <=3 ms, q3 <=12 ms, q4 <=20 ms, q6 <=60 ms; q5 no >10% regression
      from the controlled baseline. n_unique over 1M distinct U32 <=30 ms;
      distinct-string group_by/unique/join <=2 s each; 41 MB parse <=2 s at 1 thread,
      <=0.6 s at 12. Keep small-input/control medians within 10% where above clock
      resolution. Test growth at 100k/300k/1M with fixed key-length distributions;
      report time per row AND byte, rather than claim worst-case linear hashing.
  G4  Peak process RSS <=600 MB on the frozen 1M workload, including input
      preservation and output mode as specified at W0. Also measure live/retained
      storage and allocations per phase. RSS high-water marks are not subtractable
      phase costs; use isolated phase runs or interval/live-memory instrumentation.
  G5  Every changed proved production path retains its theorem/refinement chain;
      new chunked/fused kernels have content laws and --verdict passes. No @unsafe,
      foreign workaround or assumed equivalence. Existing dictionary hypotheses
      remain explicitly conditional; they are not discharged by changing layout.
  G6  Report parse, preparation copies, compute and output separately plus end-to-end
      results; record allocated/copied bytes and instrument overhead separately.
      Performance gates run in a controlled benchmark job, not noisy correctness CI.

Out of scope for v0.2: new API surface (none in this milestone), GPU, bit-packed
masks (a v0.3 item; <= 1 ms on q1/q2 today), R2-R5 as theorems (unchanged from
v0.1: optimize_full stays opt-in), F64.

1. Reconciliation of the two reviews
=====================================================================================
Agreed by both, with the same evidence: primary column storage and the group/sort
loops use flat buffers, while some nullable helpers still box per-row results;
the CSV reader is cons-list bound end to end; n_unique
takes a list path; eval clones per column reference; the join deep-copies its
table and discards the copy; the CSV column fork is a 1-vs-rest chain; nothing
forks on q1-q6; matching ANode copies both halves (blk_half / blk_node), so
naive repeated splitting/reassembly lost to the flat loop in the probes; owned
chunks amortize that cost (both reviews report about 3.8x in different synthetic
workloads). Astra measured 128 maps after one split, not a single-map engine gain.
Fable reports --verdict acceptance of its prototype; this alone is not a proved
content-equivalence theorem for a future chunk executor. F32 sums are row-ordered
and must not be reassociated silently.

2. Design decisions (validated in W0, then frozen)
=====================================================================================
D1 Owned chunks; prove the layout choice in W0 before freezing it. The sketch
     Chunks<T> = CLeaf{n, capacity/depth, Array<T>} | CNode{rows, left, right}
   covers numeric payloads, validity and Str codes; Raw strings and dictionary
   ownership also need explicit representations. Choose grain from 2^13..2^16.
   Chunk nodes are user datatypes, not ANode, so navigation does not copy arrays.
   Frame columns and validity share LOGICAL ROW BOUNDARIES. Zip/when/filter cannot
   pair arbitrary leaves by tree position. Normalize or align different chunk
   partitions, including concat, slice, literal broadcast, joins and empty leaves.
   Compaction applies one per-chunk mask to every column and retains row order;
   outputs may stay ragged but keep aligned logical boundaries. Rebalance jointly,
   by measured occupancy/work, with amortized bounds; avoid flatten/rebuild per op.
   Specify empty frames, zero-column height and padding exclusion. Legacy branchless
   compaction writes even dropped rows: if reusing it, prove cursor capacity and
   the kept+1 scratch-slot condition, or use a new guarded-write kernel. Fixed leaf
   capacity alone does not discharge CRoom. Count sums/offsets obey the row cap.
   Sort/join may flatten at a measured boundary initially; include all copies in
   timing/RSS, with a bound of one conversion per boundary, not per expression.
   Do not promise a constant 0.2 ms conversion for all dtypes/sizes.
D2 Ownership. Borrow-and-return helpers permit sequential reuse, not simultaneous
   access to one affine buffer. Plain Col/Alias projections move after static
   validation. A use/liveness analysis includes all expressions, duplicate references,
   self-join branches and with_columns' INPUT snapshot. Same-type map/zip can reuse
   a dead operand. Parallel consumers need disjoint owned chunks or costed copies;
   define that ownership before scheduling. Track copy bytes and retained capacity,
   including head(1) over a large buffer and sliced dictionaries.
D3 Fusion. Specialize scalar operands without materializing broadcasts; fuse ordinary
   null-propagating arithmetic/comparison zip with validity/reset; keep separate
   Kleene and/or rules (false AND null and true OR null remain valid). Filling nulls
   with a non-null dtype default can reuse payload bytes, but MUST set validity:
   it is not a semantic no-op. Other fills use one masked map. Fuse casts only under
   their real strict/lenient semantics and F32 rounding points; no reassociation or
   FMA contraction. Predicates can produce mask+count together, and several aligned
   columns can compact in one pass. Unboxed R{value,valid} is an experiment checked
   in emitted C, not an assumed compiler guarantee.
   Initially fuse total row-wise fragments. Preserve instruction/expression/row
   error precedence for fallible fragments: row-major fusion or task completion
   order must not choose a different strict-cast error. Add explicit counterexamples
   with competing errors in different rows, operands and chunks. Static checks run
   before effects/data work just as in v0.1. Any certified Filter+Select or Sort+Head
   physical shortcut needs its own production equivalence law, even if R2-R5 remain
   outside the release optimizer.
D4 F32 policy unchanged: one Neumaier pass in row order per column/group, including
   intermediate rounding. Fork across independent aggregates or complete groups
   only with an explicit ownership plan and preserved per-group order; skewed hot
   groups may remain serial. U32 modular sums/counts/popcount can combine by a tree.
   Min/max need proven combines under the exact NaN, signed-zero, null/all-null and
   tie policies. Preserve error ordering across parallel aggregates. Do not imply
   that node-wise combines work for all reductions or guarantee q3/q6 speedups.
D5 Strings. First-appearance HASH IDS and lexical SORT RANKS are different products.
   A common Raw coder can use presized arrays of hashes, representative-row+1 and
   id. Keep the owned backing strings alive and hand them back; hash matches MUST
   compare actual strings. Specify capacity/overflow, load factor, probe exhaustion,
   collisions and empty/null behavior. Sequential insertion establishes first-row
   ids; a parallel coder needs deterministic global first-occurrence remapping.
   Sorting still needs lexical ordering of distinct entries and a rank map; never
   use first-appearance ids as sort words. Benchmark that stage separately.
   Parallel probes cannot share a mutable affine table simply by handing it back.
   W0 must demonstrate a safe architecture: sequential borrowed probe, bounded
   per-worker copies, or owned hash partitions plus deterministic output assembly.
   Choose by measured copying, skew and throughput. No Array.fork workaround.
   Parser-private cold types and SFlat are gated by emitted-C and integration tests:
   conversion from public hot String may itself copy, so charge the full bridge.
D6 Reader. Establish globally valid record boundaries BEFORE parallel parsing. A
   chunk-local quote flag initialized outside quotes cannot tell whether its start
   is inside a quoted field. Use a sequential boundary/state scan or associative
   lexical-state summaries with prefix composition; cover doubled quotes, CRLF,
   UTF-8 splits, malformed input, and records longer than a piece. Carry physical
   line counts and offsets for deterministic errors. A fallback is entered from
   known global state, not a guessed chunk-local ending state.
   Freeze dtypes from the documented prefix; infer_rows=0 (whole input), explicit
   schemas, headerless/empty files and later conflicting values keep their contracts.
   Preserve the existing ordering among malformed/ragged/type/capacity/value errors,
   not earliest task completion. Avoid an entire second read just to report an error.
   Validate the installed File API and independent-handle ownership; do not assume
   a concurrent File.read_at primitive. A producer of owned pieces is acceptable.
   A 1-2 MB file piece can contain many 2^14-row leaves: allocate leaf builders by
   row counts/capacity, not one fixed-capacity leaf per byte-sized piece. A legacy
   per-piece parser still transposes rows; only the direct typed-builder step removes
   that cost. Merge dictionaries/remap codes in source-piece order, charge serial
   merges, test collisions/uneven cardinality, and retain conditional proof premises.
   Differential CSV tests vary piece/leaf boundaries and compete errors, not only
   happy-path files or a single quoted-newline fixture.
D7 Rollout. Freeze a runnable baseline at 3612482 and preserve its oracle artifacts.
   Define the engine selector and representation boundary in W0; the new layout and
   fusion remain opt-in until G1-G6 and clean-consumer checks pass. Compare old/new
   eager, raw lazy, default and full optimized paths across thread/chunk counts.
   Flip the default separately from removal. Remove duplicated production code only
   after a documented stabilization period, two independently reproduced full gate
   runs with the new default, and a reproducible final baseline run. Retain a pinned
   external reference runner/goldens for regression testing. W8 does
   not automatically delete the reference before the new default is validated.

3. Work packages, in order
=====================================================================================
Estimates below are provisional engineering budgets, not completion commitments;
include proof, test and benchmark work in each package.
W0 Feasibility and controlled baseline (before architecture freeze).
   Re-run the review probes and q1-q6/end-to-end at the pinned baseline on a quiet
   machine; freeze inputs/expected outputs and G2-G4 thresholds with justification.
   Measure serial fractions and copy/allocation cost. Demonstrate aligned chunk
   zip/filter, one safe parallel probe ownership design, cold-parser conversion,
   lexical ranks after hash coding, and a quote-boundary scanner with adversarial
   input. These are disposable spikes, not a second engine. Stop/revise the design
   if ownership, proof or memory assumptions fail. G6 instrumentation lands here.
W1 Cheap wins, no layout change (~2 days). Gate: ci, diff and before/after probes.
   a. join.bend:592, 635: clone the table only when Probe.levels > 0; the fork
      helper takes one owned Dict and clones only at a split. (5.1 s -> ~3.4 s on
      the distinct-key join.)
   b. n_unique: radix-sort the order words and count runs in one fold; Bool and
      small ranges via a presence array; Raw strings via W2's coder. (~200 ms ->
      ~15 ms; add a 1M-distinct test.)
   c. describe: borrow-and-return, one fused count/sum/min/max pass; fix the
      wrong clone comment. (108 -> ~55 ms.)
   d. Kernel.map_maybe / zip_maybe: unboxed R{v, ok}. (casts, floor_div, mod,
      Kleene: 4.9 -> 0.7 ms per pass.)
   e. Eval.when.valid: a 1-slot ones array instead of a materialised AllValid.
   f. Column.slice: one range copy instead of iota + gather.
   g. Sort+Head: retain the exact full argsort, gather min(k,height) rows only;
      validate keys/errors even for k=0. This is bounded gathering, NOT a top-k
      selection algorithm. Add production equivalence and stability/NaN/null tests.
   h. Extend W0 reporting with per-workload copy/allocation regressions (G6).
   All parenthesized speedups in W1-W4 are hypotheses from review probes, not gates
   already achieved; attribute reported versus independently reproduced evidence.
W2 The distinct-key coder (~3 days). Implement D5's coder and separate lexical-rank
   stage; use them in group_by/unique, Sort.str_words and join as appropriate. Verify
   first-appearance order, collision resolution and rank mapping against the baseline
   before removing pcodes/pmerge. Add long shared-prefix keys, all-equal/distinct,
   skewed cardinalities and forced-collision tests. Use W0's proven-safe ownership
   design for probes; copying cost is included. Gate: frozen G3 time/growth targets.
W3 Ownership and the sort pipeline (~3 days). D2 moves in eval and exec; sort
   builds words by reading the column in place, skips the identity xmap and the
   iota gather, computes vary + sortedness + all digit histograms in one read
   pass, ping-pongs two buffers, skips the radix for sorted words; group_by moves
   only dead aggregation inputs, and removes single-key redundant passes only after
   checking sparse ids, nulls and first-appearance order. Interim targets: q3 <=15,
   q4 <=25 ms at 1 thread; clone bytes on q1-q5 down >=50%. W0 freezes these
   alongside G3; all correctness/proof gates apply.
W4 Fusion (~4 days). D3 peepholes with their laws (see section 4). Gate: frozen G3
   q1/q2 thresholds; every fused kernel has a content law; --verdict green.
W5 Chunked columns (~6 days). D1 and D7: the type, WFChunks, the kernels'
   CNode/CLeaf wrappers (leaf lemmas reused), per-leaf compaction and rechunk,
   flatten/rebuild for sort and join, the coarse-grain forks of D4 (aggregations,
   per-column gathers, columns of a compaction, expressions of a with_columns).
   Gate: frozen G2 compute thresholds, boundary/partition differential tests, G5.
   Measure flatten/rechunk/dictionary-merge costs; no hidden copy outside the clock.
W6 The reader (~5 days). D6 in three steps: (i) piece tree + fork with today's
   per-piece parser and bounded aligned leaves (parallel, still transposes);
   (ii) the one-loop typed parser with scalar state and cold private types;
   (iii) dictionary merge across pieces in the join. Gate: G2 for parse, G3 parse
   targets, G4 RSS, CSV coverage tests and the B02 regressions green, the quoted-
   LF fallback tested with a fixture that has a newline inside quotes in every
   piece, plus all D6 boundary/error/inference tests.
W7 SFlat experiment (~3 days, may be deferred). Compare total parse+conversion+
   query time and RSS, Unicode correctness and proof cost. Adopt only for >=2x on
   the frozen distinct-key cells with <=10% Dictionary/control regression and no
   unsafe bridge; otherwise document/defer. Do not change code-point semantics.
W8 Close (~2 days, excluding stabilization). Re-measure the full table at 1/4/12
   threads and verify G1-G6 before flipping the default. Keep the reference per D7;
   removal is a later explicit gate. Update README/bench README/DESIGN 8 and 14/
   CHANGELOG with measured scope, unsupported paths and conditional proof premises.
   Re-run consumer, mutation and hosted-platform checks (or disclose unavailable
   platforms). Preserve the baseline and reproducible results. No publish implied.

Order rationale: W1-W3 remove major costs, but are NOT risk-free: each change must
audit transitive theorem callers and invariants, even when its leaf is tested-only.
W4 makes the passes fat enough that W5's forks pay (a 0.3 ms job loses to pool wake-up); W6 is
the biggest absolute win but the most new correctness obligations, so it comes
after the engine is stable; W7 is optional.

Execution guidance: do not spawn agents from this plan alone. Delegation requires
current user authorization. Every change carries reproducible before/after data,
a differential run and an audit of affected proof dependencies. Correctness and
proof gates are never weakened to recover a performance target.

=====================================================================================
4. Proof obligations created by this milestone
=====================================================================================
  - WFChunks/WFFrame: shape/occupancy, logical sizes, aligned column/validity
    boundaries, row-cap-safe sums, valid padding and dictionary ownership. Smart
    constructors establish these; transformations and adapters preserve them.
  - Bridge: logical cell addressing, flatten/rebuild round-trip and validity/order
    equality, including ragged/empty chunks and dictionaries with different codes.
  - Kernel proofs: pointwise map is a simple node congruence; zip needs alignment,
    compact needs logical concatenation and cursor bounds, gather needs arbitrary
    cross-chunk addressing, reductions need lawful combines, and scatter needs
    exclusive ownership or ordered updates. Do not treat all wrappers as congruence.
  - Fused kernels: production equivalence to unfused evaluation, including cardinality,
    masks, F32 rounding and permitted errors. Kleene logic and fill_null get their
    own validity laws. Error-prone fusion remains disabled until its ordering proof.
  - Projection/moves/in-place kernels: observation equivalence plus no mutation of
    live input snapshots; exact alias/name validation; old-input/new-output invariant.
  - Sort+Head gathering: equivalence to full sort then head for values, stable order,
    schema, k=0 and deterministic failures. This is not an exemption from G5.
  - n_unique/coder/reader remain tested-only where v0.1 was, explicitly listed in the
    assurance matrix; new low-level kernels still need G5 laws. Preserve conditional
    Dict.merge/decode laws and identify each runtime invariant supporting premises.
  - Discharge CRoom/CatFits at actual callers using fit, cursor and row-cap proofs;
    fixed leaf capacity alone is insufficient. No new assumptions may replace a
    previously proved fact, and no Probe/EncProbe obligation disappears by omission.
  - Require in-place mutation controls for new kernel/bridge rules; --verdict passing
    a well-typed prototype alone is not evidence of semantic equivalence.

=====================================================================================
5. Measurement protocol
=====================================================================================
Accept gate samples only with a quiet PRE-RUN machine (target load <2 and no known
competing jobs). A 12-thread measurement itself raises load: do not require in-run
load <2 or reject slow samples after seeing results. Predeclare acceptance/retry
rules; record all samples, CPU activity/load and thermal/power conditions. If the
host cannot be quiet, report provisional observations and leave gates unevaluated.

Pin compiler/runtime/flags, machine, datasets, string length/cardinality/skew and
thread counts. Use at least five fresh processes, interleave baseline/candidate
and 1/4/12-thread runs, report medians, dispersion and raw values. Predefine warmup
and input-preservation mode equally. For sub-ms cells batch above clock resolution
without hiding setup or inflating retained inputs; measure cold end-to-end too.
Thread scaling compares the SAME candidate workload, including its serial work,
not a microkernel with sequential preprocessing omitted.

Store command, commit+dirty/source hash, toolchain, workload hash, engine/grain,
timing boundaries, result validation and allocation/RSS method in each JSONL record
under bench/results/m2/. Timed results must remain live and be validated afterwards.
Include rows around chunk boundaries, selectivities 0/1/50/100%, narrow/wide schemas,
all-equal/distinct/skewed and long-prefix strings, many tiny chunks and repeated
queries. Limits and memory-capacity errors get separate correctness tests.

Preserve/reproduce the audits' source-backed probes under bench/micro/; do not turn
reported exploratory estimates into verified baseline facts. Include the hot-type
probe in the real imported program: separate cold microprogram wins may disappear
when a representation becomes shared through an integration boundary. Inspect
specific hot loops in emitted C and allocation counters; a global term_keep grep
is not an adequate performance or correctness gate.

=====================================================================================
6. Risks
=====================================================================================
  - The chunk grain is a guess (2^14): too small loses to scheduling, too large
    loses balance on 12 threads. Measure before committing WFChunks.
  - The reader needs globally established lexical state at every cut; local quote
    parity is insufficient. Test the boundary scan, long records and fallback; keep
    the sequential reference covered.
  - Hot-type/refcount behavior is compiler-version and whole-program dependent.
    Keep parser-private types private and charge conversion costs; verify the
    actual lowering after integration, not just a source annotation.
  - Fusion multiplies kernel variants; cap the peephole set to the list in D3 and
    require a law per variant, or the proof chain becomes the brake.
  - Dictionary merge across pieces changes codes; every consumer must go through
    the remap, and the Dictionary laws must be re-checked under per-piece build.
  - Both reviews' ratios were measured under load 10-200; G2-G4 may move once the
    machine is idle, in either direction.
